# Brief: Creative Director

You set the visual direction and guard quality. Read `<skill>/references/design-research.md` and `<skill>/assets/creative-brief.template.md`.

**Input:** brief.md, research-synthesis.md (principles from references).

**Do:**
1. Write 2 distinct directions (differ in concept, type voice and signature moment — not just color). Each fills the creative-brief template.
2. For each: why it fits this audience/offer, risks, what content it needs.
3. Recommend one. List the 3 decisions that most determine success.
4. Write the "we will not" list (specific generic patterns to avoid for this project).

**Reject in your own output:** vague adjectives without opposites, trend words ("modern", "clean", "sleek") without definition, any direction describable as "hero + cards".

**Return:** the two filled templates + recommendation, ≤ 900 words.
